01المشكلة The problem

The whole thing was built around the agent

Sara owns an apartment and rents it out. One day she'll sell it. Either way she hits the same wall: scrolling listings, guessing at prices, wondering who to trust. When she finally lists, high commission, no transparency and a fragmented market leave her frustrated. It isn't just sellers, renters, buyers and owners all hit the same wall, because the journey was built around the agent, not the people in the deal.

Too many brokers

Multiple agents on one property, each chasing a commission on a deal the buyer and seller often found themselves.

A 2% commission

An agent's cut on every transaction, the single biggest avoidable cost in a UAE property deal.

Documentation delay

Cash checks, mortgage readiness and paperwork stall the close long after a serious buyer appears.

04المرحلة الأولى Phase one

Phase 1, and what it has to prove

Phase 1 is the foundation the rest of EQARY sits on. One job: let someone rent a home or reserve an off-plan unit entirely in the app, with government-verified identity, digital contracts and bank-to-bank payment built in from day one. Win rentals and off-plan, stand up the trust layer, and start generating the transaction data the later phases depend on.

Rent with no agent and no cheque

A tenant finds a home, gets verified, signs the lease with UAE Pass and pays the landlord directly. No agent in the middle, no post-dated cheques.

Tenants & landlords

List only what you own

A landlord can only list once the property clears the ADREC ownership check, then screens an already-verified tenant and is paid straight to their account. Fake listings die at the source.

Landlords

Reserve off-plan, pre-qualified

A buyer reserves a unit from a verified developer, already identity-checked and income-qualified, and the developer gets a warm, ready lead routed in real time.

Buyers & developers

Money moves, EQARY never holds it

Rent, deposits and reservation fees move bank to bank and split mid-air, so EQARY is never in the money path. That keeps the licensing footprint light at launch.

Payments

First definition of done: 500 verified landlord listings, 50 verified developer listings, and 1,000 transactions processed end to end, live in Abu Dhabi with Dubai ready to switch on.

06نموذج العمل Business model

How EQARY makes money

Users pay a small flat fee, far less than an agent's commission. The real revenue comes from the banks and developers around the deal.

Low flat fee

No 2% commission. A small flat fee, a fraction of what an agent charges. That is how we win users over.

Developer fees

Developers pay a listing and lead-generation fee to access our verified, pre-approved buyer pipeline.

Bank referral fees

The main one. Every mortgage or loan that runs through a deal earns a referral fee from the bank.

Premium services

Photography, listing placement, buyer pipelines and dashboards for developers and landlords who want them.

Ecosystem leads & ads

Maintenance, insurance, moving and utility providers pay per qualified referral, never the user.

What each side gets

EQARY only wins when everyone in the deal does. Here is what each side walks away with.

Sellers & landlords

An investor dashboard and analytics that turns an owner into their own agent: every property, tenant, payment and yield in one place, with pricing benchmarks and occupancy at a glance. They list, screen and get paid directly, and watch the whole portfolio without ever calling a broker.

Become the new agent

Buyers & tenants

A verified, end-to-end close in one app: identity, credit and income confirmed once, contracts signed with UAE Pass, money moving bank to bank, and no 2% commission anywhere in the deal.

Banks & mortgage providers

A pipeline of pre-verified, income-confirmed, credit-checked borrowers, routed at the moment they're transacting. Higher intent than any portal, and the main driver of EQARY's revenue.

RNPL partners

Applicants already gated by AECB credit and open-banking income, so the hardest financing piece arrives de-risked and ready to underwrite.

Service providers

Maintenance, insurance, movers and utilities get qualified, per-lead referrals at the moment of move-in or handover, paid by the provider, never the user.
