01 Overview

Digitizing the real estate market

EQARY puts buyers, sellers, landlords and developers in one app. Identity verified against UAE Pass, contracts signed in-app, money moving bank to bank.

01The problem

The whole thing was built around the agent

Too many brokers

Multiple agents on one property, each chasing a commission on a deal the buyer and seller often found themselves.

A 2% commission

An agent's cut on every transaction, the single biggest avoidable cost in a UAE property deal.

Documentation delay

Cash checks, mortgage readiness and paperwork stall the close long after a serious buyer appears.

06Architecture

How the whole system fits together

EQARY is an orchestration layer that coordinates the transaction across regulated providers. Money moves bank to bank, with the platform fee split off in the same movement, so EQARY never holds it. That keeps the licensing footprint light, with the exact perimeter confirmed by counsel.
